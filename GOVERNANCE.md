# Governance

OpenSharing is a Series of LF Projects, LLC, hosted by the LF AI & Data
Foundation. Its governance is defined authoritatively by the project's
**Technical Charter**, adopted **June 9, 2026**.

## Technical Charter

The full Technical Charter is committed in this repository:

- [`CHARTER.md`](./CHARTER.md) — readable Markdown transcription
- [`charter/OpenSharing-Technical-Charter-2026-06-09.pdf`](./charter/OpenSharing-Technical-Charter-2026-06-09.pdf) — the executed, signed copy of record

The summary below is for convenience; where it differs from the Charter, the
Charter governs.

## Technical Steering Committee (TSC)

- The **TSC** is responsible for all technical oversight of the project
  (Charter §2.a).
- **Voting members** are the project's **Maintainers**, listed in
  [MAINTAINERS.md](./MAINTAINERS.md) (Charter §2.b).
- The TSC may **elect a Chair** to preside over meetings (Charter §2.f).
- TSC responsibilities include the technical direction of the project, approving
  proposals, establishing community norms and release/security policies, and
  coordinating with other communities (Charter §2.g).
- TSC **meetings are open to the public** and may be held electronically, by
  teleconference, or in person (Charter §2.b).

## Voting

- One vote per voting member (Charter §3.a).
- **Quorum** is at least 50% of voting members (Charter §3.b).
- Most decisions require a **majority** of those in attendance, quorum met
  (Charter §3.c).
- Amending the Charter, or granting a license exception, requires a **two-thirds**
  vote of the entire TSC (Charter §7.c, §8.a).

## Roles

- **Contributor → Maintainer** by majority TSC approval; removal by majority TSC
  approval (Charter §2.c). Roles are documented in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Licensing and IP

- Inbound and outbound **code**: Apache-2.0 (Charter §7.b).
- **Documentation**: CC-BY-4.0; **data**: CDLA-Permissive-2.0 (Charter §7.b.iv).
- **DCO** sign-off required on all contributions (Charter §7.b.ii).
- Copyright is retained by contributors; no CLA/copyright assignment is required
  (Charter §7.a).

## Open participation

The project allows open participation from any individual or organization meeting
the contribution requirements, **regardless of competitive interest** (Charter
§4.d), and operates transparently (Charter §4.e).
