# Maintainers

This file lists the current Maintainers of OpenSharing. Per the
[Technical Charter](./GOVERNANCE.md) (§2.b), **the Maintainers are the voting
members of the Technical Steering Committee (TSC)**.

Roles and the promotion process are defined in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md): a Contributor becomes a Maintainer by a
majority vote of the TSC.
